About Calvary Orphanage Uganda

Calvary Orphanage Uganda was founded to give hope to vulnerable children who have lost parents or come from broken homes.

We provide food, shelter, education, and God's love to over 30 children in Uganda.

Every child deserves a chance to dream, learn, and grow. Your support helps us buy food, school books, clothes, and medicine.

"Proverbs 19:17 - Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the Lord." Thank you for being His hands and feet.
